## Requirements

### Requirement: BrowserCookieValidation

The system SHALL validate BrowserCookie according to these rules:
- Name cannot be empty
- sourcePort must be -1 or between 1-65535
- expires must be a valid timestamp for persistent cookies

#### Scenario: SetCookie
- **GIVEN** A website that requires authentication
- **WHEN** setCookie() is called with valid name, value, and domain
- **THEN** Cookie is stored in browser and sent with subsequent requests to matching domain

#### Scenario: DeleteCookie
- **GIVEN** A cookie exists in the browser
- **WHEN** deleteCookies() is called with matching name and url/domain
- **THEN** Cookie is removed from browser storage

#### Scenario: ClearAllCookies
- **GIVEN** Browser has multiple cookies
- **WHEN** clearBrowserCookies() is called
- **THEN** All cookies are removed from the browser

#### Scenario: RetrieveCookiesForUrl
- **GIVEN** Browser has cookies for various domains
- **WHEN** getCookies() is called with specific URLs
- **THEN** Only cookies applicable to those URLs are returned
